# JDGF Review and Cognitive Testing Pack

This directory contains the controlled materials required to move JDGF from documentary development into structured scientific review and pre-pilot testing.

## Contents

- `JDGF-Expert-Review-Packet.md` — multidisciplinary review form covering the framework, dimensions, maturity model, instrument, critical controls, context, and fairness.
- `JDGF-Cognitive-Interview-Guide.md` — think-aloud and probing protocol for testing item interpretation, evidence retrieval, scoring, applicability, and burden.
- `JDGF-Review-and-Revision-Log.md` — permanent traceability register for comments, decisions, scoring changes, critical controls, terminology, and implementation verification.
- `JDGF-Expert-Review-Report-Template.md` — structured report for expert-review findings and decisions.
- `JDGF-Cognitive-Testing-Report-Template.md` — structured report for response-process findings and readiness for institutional piloting.

## Required sequence

1. Freeze the reviewed JDGF version and identify its commit SHA.
2. Obtain reviewer declarations and required ethics or institutional approvals.
3. Conduct independent expert review.
4. Record and approve revisions in the review log.
5. Implement and independently verify approved revisions.
6. Conduct cognitive interviews on the revised instrument.
7. Resolve critical and high-severity comprehension or evidence problems.
8. Issue the cognitive-testing report.
9. Authorize the first institutional pilot only after the documented readiness decision.

## Status rule

Completion of these materials does not validate JDGF. Validation claims require actual review participants, authorized institutional fieldwork, independently collected evidence, agreement analysis, and documented release decisions.
