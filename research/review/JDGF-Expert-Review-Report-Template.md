## 1. Executive summary

Summarize:

- purpose and scope of the review;
- panel composition;
- materials reviewed;
- principal strengths;
- critical and high-priority concerns;
- overall recommendation;
- revisions required before cognitive testing or institutional piloting.

## 2. Review objectives

The review should determine whether the JDGF v1.0 candidate is:

- conceptually coherent;
- sufficiently complete;
- legally, ethically, and institutionally appropriate;
- understandable and operationalizable;
- proportionate across institution types and resource levels;
- suitable for cognitive testing and pilot use;
- explicit about uncertainty, limitations, and non-validation.
