# JDGF Canonical Bibliography and Evidence Register

**Version:** 1.0 Candidate  
**Status:** Working bibliography — verification and systematic-review completion pending  
**Relationship:** This file is the canonical bibliographic register. `docs/08-references/JDGF-References-and-Standards-Mapping.md` provides the dimension-level crosswalk and should cite identifiers from this register rather than duplicate full annotations.

## 1. Purpose

This bibliography records normative standards, institutional guidance, scholarly research, professional bodies of knowledge, and comparative case material relevant to the Judicial Data Governance Framework (JDGF).

It supports:

- the JDGF conceptual foundation;
- the eight governance dimensions;
- the formative maturity model;
- the assessment instrument;
- the African and Global South contextual analysis;
- the scientific validation programme.

Inclusion does not imply endorsement, equivalence, certification, or unrestricted permission to reproduce protected material.

## 2. Evidence classes and verification status

Each entry should be classified as one of:

- **NORM** — normative standard or formal recommendation;
- **INST** — institutional guidance or report;
- **SCH** — scholarly or peer-reviewed research;
- **PROF** — professional body of knowledge or practitioner guidance;
- **CASE** — comparative institutional case material.

Verification status:

- **V1 — bibliographic identity verified**;
- **V2 — public summary or full text reviewed for relevance**;
- **V3 — exact proposition or clause mapped to a JDGF requirement**.

Most entries below are currently V1 or V2. V3 mapping remains part of the clause-level backlog.

## 11. Source-quality safeguards

The bibliography may include professional and comparative sources to understand practice, but JDGF claims must prioritize:

1. applicable law and official legal instruments;
2. published standards and formal recommendations;
3. peer-reviewed scholarship;
4. authoritative institutional research;
5. professional or vendor material only for clearly labelled operational illustration.

Vendor or advocacy material must never be the sole support for a normative JDGF requirement.

## 12. Systematic-review backlog

Before stable release, the review must document:

- databases and repositories searched;
- exact search strings and dates;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- deduplication and screening decisions;
- quality appraisal;
- language and jurisdiction coverage;
- African and Global South evidence coverage;
- extraction fields and synthesis method;
- conflicts and evidence gaps;
- update and version-control procedure.

Priority gaps include:

- Francophone African judicial administration;
- court, prosecution, prison, and legal-aid data governance;
- empirical studies of judicial data quality;
- records and evidentiary integrity in hybrid paper–digital environments;
- governance capacity in small and resource-constrained courts;
- independent evaluations of judicial AI systems;
- validated public-sector data-governance maturity instruments.

## 13. Maintenance rule

Every normative JDGF requirement should ultimately reference one or more bibliography identifiers and record whether the relationship is **aligned**, **adapted**, **extended**, or **distinct**. Entries with incomplete metadata must be resolved or removed before the stable v1.0 release.
