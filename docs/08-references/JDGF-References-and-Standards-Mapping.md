# JDGF References and Standards Mapping

**Version:** 1.0 Working Draft  
**Status:** Initial mapping; full literature review required

## 1. Purpose

This document records the principal bodies of knowledge, standards, and institutional references relevant to JDGF. It supports traceability and comparison; it does not imply certification, endorsement, or equivalence.

## 2. Reference families

### Data governance and management

- DAMA International, *DAMA-DMBOK: Data Management Body of Knowledge*;
- EDM Council, DCAM and related data-management capability resources;
- ISO/IEC 38505 series on governance of data;
- ISO 8000 series on data quality and master data.

### Governance, risk, and control

- COBIT governance and management objectives;
- ISO 31000 risk-management guidance;
- ISO 37301 compliance-management systems;
- ISO 37000 governance of organizations.

### Information security, privacy, and continuity

- ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002;
- ISO/IEC 27701 privacy information management;
- ISO 22301 business continuity management;
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework and relevant NIST privacy resources.

### Records, archives, and evidentiary integrity

- ISO 15489 records management;
- ISO 30301 management systems for records;
- applicable archival, evidence, procedure, and public-records law;
- professional archival and digital-preservation guidance.

### Quality, metadata, and interoperability

- ISO/IEC 11179 metadata registries;
- ISO 25012 data quality model;
- W3C and other open standards where relevant to controlled data exchange;
- sector-specific semantic and interoperability standards applicable to justice systems.

### Statistics and public transparency

- United Nations Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics;
- national statistical quality frameworks;
- open-data principles subject to confidentiality, rights, and judicial constraints;
- judicial performance and statistical guidance issued by competent regional or international bodies.

### Artificial intelligence and responsible innovation

- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence;
- OECD AI Principles;
- ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems;
- ISO/IEC 23894 AI risk management;
- applicable national and regional AI, data-protection, and digital-governance rules.

### Justice-sector governance

- constitutional and statutory rules governing judicial independence, due process, access to justice, confidentiality, evidence, procedure, archives, public information, and institutional accountability;
- instruments and guidance issued by competent judicial, regional, and international organizations.

## 3. Initial JDGF mapping

| JDGF dimension | Principal reference families |
|---|---|
| Leadership and institutional governance | ISO 37000, ISO/IEC 38505, COBIT, DAMA-DMBOK, compliance and risk frameworks |
| Legal, ethical, and rights-based governance | constitutional and procedural law, privacy law, UNESCO AI ethics, OECD AI Principles, ISO/IEC 42001 |
| Data architecture and interoperability | DAMA-DMBOK, ISO/IEC 11179, architecture and interoperability standards |
| Data quality and metadata | ISO 8000, ISO 25012, ISO/IEC 11179, DAMA-DMBOK |
| Security, privacy, and continuity | ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701, ISO 22301, NIST frameworks |
| Data lifecycle and records governance | ISO 15489, ISO 30301, archival and evidence requirements |
| Use, statistics, transparency, and responsible innovation | official-statistics principles, open-data guidance, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, judicial-sector requirements |

## 4. Mapping method

For each JDGF requirement, the full mapping should record:

- JDGF requirement identifier;
- source reference and edition;
- source clause, principle, or section;
- relationship type: aligned, adapted, extended, or distinct;
- judicial-sector rationale;
- jurisdictional limitations;
- validation status.

## 5. Scientific review requirements

Before stable release, the reference base must be strengthened through:

- a documented search strategy;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- peer-reviewed research on judicial administration, data governance, public-sector governance, records, statistics, security, privacy, and responsible AI;
- comparison across legal and institutional traditions;
- explicit treatment of African judicial and public-administration contexts;
- bibliographic verification and consistent citation style;
- review by subject-matter experts.

## 6. Limitations

This initial mapping identifies relevant reference families but is not yet a systematic literature review or clause-level crosswalk. Exact editions and applicability must be verified before publication of JDGF v1.0.
