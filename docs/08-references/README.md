# JDGF References

## Purpose

This directory records the scientific, legal, standards-based, and institutional sources used to justify JDGF design decisions.

## Reference categories

- judicial administration and justice-sector reform;
- public-sector and institutional governance;
- data governance and data management;
- data quality, metadata, records, archives, and interoperability;
- privacy, confidentiality, cybersecurity, and fundamental rights;
- statistics and evidence-based public management;
- digital transformation and responsible AI governance;
- African legal and institutional contexts.

## Minimum source record

For each source, record:

- full citation;
- source type;
- jurisdiction or institutional context;
- JDGF concept or decision supported;
- relevant notes or limitations;
- date accessed where applicable.

## Quality rules

Prefer peer-reviewed literature, official standards, legislation, authoritative institutional guidance, and empirical studies. Framework elements must not be presented as internationally established merely because they resemble common practice. Distinguish adopted external concepts from JDGF-specific proposals.

## Initial reference families requiring formal review

- DAMA-DMBOK;
- ISO data-quality, security, privacy, records, and AI governance standards;
- COBIT and public-sector governance literature;
- OECD, World Bank, UN, African Union, and regional justice-sector guidance;
- applicable national, regional, and international legal frameworks;
- empirical scholarship on African judicial administration and digital transformation.

A validated bibliography has not yet been completed.
