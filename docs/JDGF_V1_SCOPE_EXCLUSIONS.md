# JDGF v1.0 Scope Exclusions

**Status:** Adopted scope boundary for the v1.0 candidate  
**Date:** 2026-07-28

## 1. Included in JDGF v1.0

JDGF v1.0 includes:

- the foundations and theory of change;
- the eight governance dimensions;
- the formative maturity model;
- evidence and confidence requirements;
- the documentary assessment methodology;
- the paper or Markdown assessment questionnaire;
- implementation guidance;
- the glossary;
- standards and references mapping;
- research and validation protocols;
- illustrative, clearly labelled simulated cases.

The documentary assessment questionnaire is included because it operationalizes the maturity model and supports consistent, evidence-based application.

## 2. Excluded from JDGF v1.0

The following are outside the normative scope of v1.0:

- a JDGAT software application or hosted platform;
- automated evidence collection or ingestion;
- automated maturity scoring or recommendations;
- dashboards presented as an authorized certification product;
- algorithmic conformity or certification decisions;
- integration connectors to court information systems;
- production AI models or judicial decision-support software;
- an accredited certification scheme;
- claims of empirical validity before completion of the approved validation programme.

## 3. Naming boundary

`JDGAT` should be reserved for a future digital assessment tool or platform. The v1.0 questionnaire is referred to as the **JDGF Assessment Instrument**.

## 4. Validation boundary

Inclusion in the v1.0 candidate does not mean that the instrument is empirically validated. Until the validation plan is completed, all outputs must state that the framework and scoring system are methodologically specified and pre-validation.

## 5. Future extensions

Excluded products may be developed in later work packages or releases only with separate requirements, security, privacy, usability, governance, validation, and change-control processes.
