# JDGF Glossary

## Status

**Controlled vocabulary — working draft**

This glossary is the authoritative location for JDGF terminology. Other documents should link here instead of redefining terms independently.

## Initial terms

### Data governance

The system of institutional authority, responsibilities, policies, processes, controls, capabilities, and evidence through which data-related decisions are made and monitored.

### Judicial data

Data created, received, maintained, exchanged, or used by institutions performing judicial, prosecutorial, registry, correctional, administrative, statistical, oversight, or related justice-sector functions.

### Data owner

The institutionally accountable role authorized to make or approve decisions concerning a defined data domain, subject to law, judicial mandates, and governance arrangements.

### Data steward

A designated role responsible for coordinating and maintaining agreed data definitions, quality practices, metadata, controls, and issue resolution for a data domain.

### Data quality

The degree to which data are fit for their authorized judicial, administrative, operational, statistical, or research purposes.

### Metadata

Structured information describing data meaning, origin, context, format, ownership, transformations, controls, and permitted use.

### Data lineage

Documented information about the origin, movement, transformation, aggregation, and use of data across processes and systems.

### Maturity

The demonstrated and sustained institutional capability to perform defined governance practices with appropriate responsibility, evidence, monitoring, and improvement.

### Responsible AI readiness

The institutional state in which governance, data quality, rights safeguards, accountability, oversight, competencies, and risk controls are sufficiently developed to evaluate and, where justified, govern AI-enabled uses.

## Editorial rule

Every future term should include its definition, boundaries, related terms, and source or rationale where relevant.
